Diet Detectives: The Lipid Files

Year 8 Science · Lipids · 40 minutes

Before you start

Playing fair

  • The worksheet runs in full screen. It stays that way until you submit.
  • Leaving it (switching tab, app, window or screen, or leaving full screen) counts as an exit. Every exit is recorded with the time and your teacher sees all of them.
  • From your third exit, your answer boxes lock for 3 minutes each time you leave.
  • Copying and pasting are switched off. Type every answer yourself.
  • Your work saves automatically. Press Submit at the end of the lesson.

Your mission

Fats and oils (lipids) have a bad reputation. In these six files, some patients eat too much, some eat too little, and some eat the wrong kind. One case is not even a patient. Your job is to work out what is going on.

Read each file carefully and answer every question in full sentences, using the science words from your lessons. Show your working for any calculation.

Aim for about 6 minutes per case. Finished early? Try the Bonus task.
