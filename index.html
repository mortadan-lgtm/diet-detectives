Before you start

Playing fair

  • The worksheet runs in full screen. It stays that way until you submit.
  • Leaving it (switching tab, app, window or screen, or leaving full screen) counts as an exit. Every exit is recorded with the time and your teacher sees all of them.
  • From your third exit, your answer boxes lock for 3 minutes each time you leave.
  • Copying and pasting are switched off. Type every answer yourself.
  • Your work saves automatically. Press Submit at the end of the lesson.

Your mission

You are a trainee nutritionist at a busy clinic. Six patients have arrived, and every one of them has a health problem caused by their diet. Read each patient file, study the symptoms and the food diary, and crack the case.

For every case you will answer four questions: Diagnosis, Evidence, The science and Prescription. Write in full sentences and use the science words from your lessons.

Aim for about 5 minutes per case. Finished early? Try the Bonus Case, then check your answers.
